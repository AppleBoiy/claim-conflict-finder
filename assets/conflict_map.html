Evidence matrix

Each row is a relationship that two or more papers make a claim about; each column is a paper, oldest on the left. Marks read against the row's wording: ▲ under “A outperforms B” means the paper agrees; ▼ means it found the opposite. Click a mark to read the claim.

Where papers disagree

Pairs are ranked by importance. Genuine means the two papers report the same kind of setup yet reach different answers — read both. The others are probably explained by a difference in context, highlighted in yellow.
